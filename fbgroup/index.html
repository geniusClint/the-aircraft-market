Inside the group

What you get from the group

One feed for the whole piston market, instead of checking four different sites and a dozen groups yourself.

01

Fresh listings, early

New piston listings as they surface across Marketplace, Facebook groups, Trade-A-Plane, and Controller — with asking price, total time, and engine time (SMOH / STOH) when the seller lists them, and a link straight to the original listing.

02

Price drops & standout deals

When a seller cuts the price, it gets flagged. So do the listings priced well under comparable planes — the ones that usually disappear before they ever reach the big listing sites' front pages.

03

What left the market

Keep up to date with what planes are on the market and the ones that have sold or left the market — so you learn what real asking prices actually do, not just what sellers hope for.

04

Other buyers & sellers, not tire-kickers

Ask about a model you're eyeing, compare notes on hours and engine times, and talk pricing with people who are actually in the piston market right now.
